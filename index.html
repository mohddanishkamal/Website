Academic & Professional Background

Mohd Danish Kamal is an Indian author and creative strategist from Meerut, Uttar Pradesh. He completed his schooling at Meerut Public School, Meerut, and went on to pursue higher education with a sustained focus on writing, ideas, and content-led initiatives.

He completed his graduation from NAS College, Meerut, followed by a Master’s Degree from Jamia Hamdard University, New Delhi.

Writing & Professional Work

Literary Contributions

Formal entry into publishing was established with the debut book, Shadows of Last Thought, released worldwide on 24 November 2024. The work marked his transition into authorship with international availability.

Alongside this debut, the body of work includes short narratives, conceptual story writing, and ongoing written projects developed across multiple formats. The approach remains concept-driven, with an emphasis on idea formation, narrative direction, and structured storytelling. This exploration of the creative process is further documented through his official blog, where narrative insights and ongoing developments are shared regularly.

Creative Vision

Inkscribers Platform

In parallel with his authorship, Kamal founded Inkscribers, a platform created to enable new and emerging voices to enter the creative and publishing space with clarity and direction.

Structured Process

Designed to help individuals move from idea development to public release through a guided framework.

Supporting Debuts

Focusing on enabling first-time creators to establish their presence and navigate early challenges.

Creative Empowerment

Championing a mission to bring work forward in an organized, practical, and impactful manner.

The platform provides the necessary guidance to transform creative projects into professional public releases.
