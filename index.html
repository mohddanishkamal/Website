Background

Mohd Danish Kamal is an Indian author and creative strategist based in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh. He completed his schooling at Meerut Public School, Meerut, and his graduation from NAS College, Meerut.

Writing

His writing includes the book Shadows of Last Thought and other works such as Pernia. He continues to write and maintain ongoing writing projects on his official blog.

The Platform

Inkscribers

Alongside his creative work, Mohd Danish Kamal founded Inkscribers, a platform created to support new and first-time writers working toward their debut. The platform exists to provide structure and direction for individuals navigating the early stages of creative and publishing work.

Inkscribers supports emerging voices as they move from initial ideas to completed work through an organized and practical framework.

Selected Work

She lives in Afghanistan, moving through her days while carrying much of her life inward. Thoughts remain unspoken, feelings unnamed. Nothing seems to break apart around her, yet something essential gives way within—

آنچه فرو ریخت، جهان نبود؛ جای او در آن بود.

What collapsed was not the world, but her place within it.

The spaces she knows remain unchanged. Walls stand where they always have, rooms hold their familiar shapes, but something no longer responds to her presence. The silence settles differently now—

خانه همان بود، اما سکوتش دیگر آشنا نبود.

The house was the same, but its silence was no longer familiar.

She does not speak of what takes root in her life. Days continue, choices are made, and meaning gathers quietly in what is left unsaid. Some things are simply carried forward—

بعضی چیزها گفته نمی‌شوند، اما تمام زندگی را شکل می‌دهند.

Some things are never spoken, yet they shape an entire life.
